# Privacy Policy — Pinterest MCP Integration

**Last updated: August 17, 2026**

## 1. Overview

Pinterest MCP Integration is a private integration used by the account owner to access and manage content from their own Pinterest Business account through the Pinterest API and an MCP-compatible assistant.

The integration is intended for the account owner's own use. It is not currently offered as a public service to third-party Pinterest users.

## 2. Information the integration may access

When authorized by the account owner, the integration may access information made available through the Pinterest API and permitted by the scopes granted to the application, including:

- Pinterest account and profile information necessary to identify the authorized account;
- boards and board metadata;
- Pins and Pin metadata; and
- other Pinterest information explicitly authorized by the account owner and required for requested functionality.

The integration does not request or collect the account owner's Pinterest password or session cookies.

## 3. How Pinterest information is used

Pinterest information is used only to provide functionality requested by the account owner, such as:

- listing or retrieving the account owner's boards and Pins;
- displaying Pinterest information back to the account owner;
- organizing or managing Pinterest content when explicitly requested; and
- performing other Pinterest API actions specifically initiated by the account owner.

The integration is not designed to perform Pinterest actions without the account owner's knowledge or instruction.

## 4. Storage and retention

Pinterest content accessed through the Pinterest API is not intended to be stored persistently by this integration. The integration is designed to request current information from Pinterest when it is needed to perform an authorized task.

Authentication credentials, such as access tokens, may be stored securely only for as long as necessary to authenticate authorized API requests. Credentials can be revoked or removed when the integration is disabled.

Any limited technical or operational information required to run the integration is retained only for as long as necessary for that purpose and is not used to build advertising profiles or unrelated datasets.

## 5. Sharing and sale of information

Pinterest information is not sold, rented, licensed, or used for third-party advertising.

Pinterest information is not intentionally disclosed to unrelated third parties. Where infrastructure or service providers are technically necessary to operate the integration, they may process limited information solely to provide that infrastructure or service and not for independent advertising or data-broker purposes.

## 6. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect access credentials and the integration against unauthorized access. API credentials and access tokens are treated as confidential and are not intentionally exposed publicly.

## 7. Account control, revocation, and deletion

The account owner controls access to Pinterest information and may revoke the integration's Pinterest authorization at any time through Pinterest account or developer settings.

The account owner may also request deletion of any locally retained authentication or operational information associated with the integration.

## 8. Legal basis and privacy rights

The integration processes information only at the direction of, and for the benefit of, the Pinterest account owner who has authorized access.

Where applicable privacy law grants rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection, requests concerning information handled by this integration may be submitted using the contact method below.

## 9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the integration's functionality or applicable requirements change. The latest version will remain available at this location, with the date of the most recent update shown above.
